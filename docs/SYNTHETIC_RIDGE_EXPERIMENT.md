# Continuous-ridge candidate: bounded synthetic comparison

Authorized October 13, 2026 after the completed failure diagnosis. Implement and measure one isolated replacement candidate. Production counting, old experiment source, rules and outputs remain unchanged. No patient processing, review application, model training, new dependency or external provider is part of this step. Stop after one frozen candidate and its repeatability run; do not tune after seeing evaluation results.

## Purpose and outputs

The prior candidate missed 20 pairs: twelve lacked separate proposals and eight were merged after proposal generation. This candidate changes both mechanisms and explicitly withholds a count when the image does not establish how proposed spaces should be grouped. Proposed boundaries and an available experimental count are different outputs. Every proposal is uncertain; clinical accuracy is always `NOT_MEASURED`.

The method is `continuous-ridge-withholding-v1`, implemented only in `scripts/ridge_counting_candidate.py`. Its detector inputs are HU, a supplied boolean ROI and an orthogonal affine. Reference labels, cores, case names, material identities, desired counts and stress metadata are available only to scoring. The supplied region bypasses anatomical localization.

## Fixed algorithm

1. Validate finite real 3D HU, matching nonempty boolean ROI and orthogonal affine. Original HU at or above 200 defines strong bone. Eligible space is the ROI below 200. Use face connectivity throughout.
2. Smooth a float64 copy of HU with a reflecting Gaussian of physical sigma equal to half the smallest voxel spacing; convert sigma separately for each array axis. Find connected `h_minima` of depth 100 HU inside eligible space. Set the exterior to the smoothed maximum plus 100 for marker detection. Give each otherwise unseeded eligible component a minimum-HU seed. More than 128 markers is an explicit resource-limit abstention.
3. Watershed the smoothed HU minus 0.001 HU/mm times padded free-space distance, using the markers and eligible mask. The small distance term orders flat interiors. These operations use the installed [h-minima](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.morphology.html#skimage.morphology.h_minima) and [watershed](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.segmentation.html#skimage.segmentation.watershed) implementations.
4. For each original adjacent basin pair, measure the 20th percentile of smoothed HU in each basin. Passage voxels are within their union and at or below the smaller percentile plus 100 HU. Compute padded physical distance to the passage boundary. Require a face-connected passage with **radius at least 1.01 times the largest voxel spacing** that touches both basin interiors. An interior lies beyond that distance from original strong-bone/ROI boundaries and at or below its basin percentile. Compute all qualifying edges against the original partition, then contract their connected groups, retaining measured intensity and radius evidence. Do not recompute percentiles on merged masks: that made count availability depend on array traversal in a three-basin unit test. Radius is not diameter; this is a fixed engineering resolution convention, not proof of anatomical communication.
5. Run the unchanged strong-bone separator routine on the remaining partition only as a support diagnostic. If it would collapse multiple proposals, withhold for `material_ambiguity`. If multiple proposed partitions remain in one original non-bone connected component, also withhold for `unresolved_communication`. Strong local plate support alone cannot settle a real opening versus an apparent imaging gap. Preserve proposed partitions in either case.
6. Apply geometric acceptance to each proposed partition using the existing configuration's minimum size, strong-bone enclosure and unsupported ROI-contact requirements. A partition must have a physical interior radius greater than 1.01 times the largest spacing. Only original HU >=200 supplies enclosure evidence; artificial proposal boundaries do not. Rejecting any partition withholds the overall count rather than silently reducing it. Record all rejected partition IDs.
7. Assign retained proposal IDs by physical centroid, calculate side from RAS X, and calculate air exclusively from original HU below -500. Retain opacity, enclosure, size, boundary and laterality reasons. Every cell receives the experimental review reason. Available experimental totals count retained proposals; withheld left/right/total values are null. No failure may become a numeric zero count.

The final geometric check is deliberately implemented in the isolated candidate: calling the old full compartment stage per partition would run the old proposal/merge algorithm again and could undo a resolved passage merge. Shared geometry/configuration and the strong-separator diagnostic are reused. Production code is byte-for-byte unchanged.

## Inputs and independent scoring

Use the 56 exact saved input sets and method results from the completed faint-wall `run-01`. Read and hash them; do not regenerate them or rerun their baselines. All 56 are now known development cases, not a new challenge or holdout. The prior faint candidate recovered 14/34 pairs and 21/22 controls.

Add exactly twelve independently constructed stress cases from `ridge_stress_cases.py`: six base cases and their exact physical axis swap/reflection. The base spacing is (0.5, 0.6, 0.8) mm and shape is (41, 31, 25). Four base cases are indistinguishable material twins at 35 and 100 HU. For each pair, a subvoxel bone sheet with fraction `(HU + 900) / 1700` of a 0.5 mm voxel yields exactly the same observed HU as a full voxel of that soft content. One latent construction has two spaces; the other has one. Both must produce identical inference and withhold their count. This demonstrates ideal averaging ambiguity, not its frequency in clinical scans.

The other two base cases contain a real 2.4 by 2.4 mm aperture or an isolated 0.5 by 2.1 by 2.2 mm fragment. Their physical predicates declare one connected space. The transformed cases swap X/Z array axes and reverse Y with the matching affine. These are reindexings of the same physical objects, not six additional independent observations. There is no new oblique resampling or scanner-calibrated blur model. Reference centers, conservative 1 mm cores and independent 2x topology checks are generated without invoking the detector. Only the material twins use exact central-voxel averaging; the aperture and fragment use declared analytic center sampling.

Reuse the unchanged scorer: complete one-to-one matching, exact total, Dice >=0.90, core coverage >=0.95, connected instances, physical side assignment for pairs and the existing control flag requirement. Report **proposal recovery** separately from **accepted experimental count recovery**; the latter additionally requires an available count. Null failures retain all reference objects with zero matches. No abstention is a recovered count.

## Admission and stop

Strict count admission requires all of: complete unique 68-case coverage; accepted paired recovery greater than the prior candidate's 14/34; every prior candidate success preserved as accepted full recovery; accepted full recovery on all 22 known controls and all four fresh aperture/fragment cases; all eight twin cases withheld with identical inference within each same-input pair; zero accepted extra divisions of one-space controls; and zero unexpected execution or input-mutation errors.

Also report whether proposal recovery improves beyond 14/34 and preserves prior proposal successes. This is a mechanism result only and cannot override failed count-admission gates. The conservative material/opening abstention rules may improve proposals while reducing accepted recovery. Do not relax the admission criteria to conceal that tradeoff, and do not promote the candidate on that basis.
