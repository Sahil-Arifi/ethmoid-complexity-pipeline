# Why correctly outlined pairs still have withheld counts

The automatic counter remains unready. The user explicitly requires automatic accuracy checks before website work; readiness for a research-review interface does not satisfy that instruction. This diagnosis does not promote the candidate or relax the completed experiment's rules.

## Measured causes

All 68 unchanged candidate replays exactly matched the saved partition, cavity and air arrays, registries, warnings and diagnostics. All 430 protected file hashes remained unchanged. Among the 34 known pairs:

| Outcome | Cases | Cause |

There are no rejected geometric proposals among those 28 withheld pairs. Correct reference outlines do not establish that the detector can identify the dividing material or distinguish an actual opening from an imaging gap.

The proposal method already applies its smoothing, minima detection and watershed in 3D. Adjacent-slice continuity is not a new independent observation. The soft-sheet controls extend through the enclosure in both tangential directions, and the material twins have identical entire HU volumes, regions and physical geometry despite opposite latent references. Any method using only those identical inputs must return the same result for both twins.

## Counterexamples to relaxing acceptance

Logical removal of the material-ambiguity veto alone makes no additional count available. Removal of the communication veto alone admits one more known pair. Removing both admits 40 previously withheld cases: 28 pairs, four known soft sheets and eight material twins. Eight of those proposed totals are wrong. This is a diagnostic calculation on saved outputs, not a newly evaluated algorithm.

Three separately declared analytic volumes isolate the communication rule: a sealed 1 mm bone wall, that wall with a 0.5 mm square opening, and that wall with a 3 mm square opening. They contain two, one and one connected physical spaces respectively. The unchanged method accepts two for the sealed wall, withholds the narrow opening and accepts one for the wider opening.

The narrow-opening example has **100% local strong-bone separator support**, but still produces two proposals. Its communication warning is the only count veto. Removing that warning would incorrectly report two cells where the construction contains one connected space. Absence of a sufficiently wide low-intensity passage is not evidence that a wall is closed. These are analytic engineering examples, not measurements of scanner resolution or clinical opening size.

Two added regression checks preserve the narrow-opening abstention in native and reflected array layouts. The detector, previous tests, inputs, scoring rules and historical outputs remain unchanged. A future acceptance change must address the evidence itself and retain this falsifier; simply lowering the pass requirements is unsupported.
